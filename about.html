About Botronix Labs

Botronix Labs is dedicated to making robotics and coding accessible to everyone. Our mission is to empower students, educators, and hobbyists with hands-on learning resources, open-source projects, and step-by-step tutorials.

What We Offer

  • Free robotics courses for all skill levels
  • 3D-printable robot designs and downloadable resources
  • Video tutorials and project-based learning
  • Open-source code and community support

Our Values

  • Accessibility: Learning should be free and open to all
  • Creativity: We encourage experimentation and innovation
  • Community: Collaboration and sharing knowledge
  • Integrity: Honest, high-quality educational content
