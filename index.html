Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a course?

Enrollment is easy! You can scan our WeChat or WhatsApp QR codes for instant support, email us directly at info@botronixlabs.com, or simply leave a message using the form on this page. Our team will guide you through the registration process and session scheduling.

What hardware is required?

For most beginner courses, we provide all kits. For advanced courses, we recommend having your own Raspberry Pi or VEX kit, which we can help you source.

Are there any prerequisites?

Our fundamental courses require no prior experience. For Intermediate and VEX programs, basic computer literacy is helpful.
